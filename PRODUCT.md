# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Diners in Ho Chi Minh City, mostly on their phones in the late afternoon, deciding where to spend a slow evening with a friend, a date or family. They want to see what the place feels like, read the menu, check it is open, and book a table without friction.

## Product Purpose
Per-branch websites for House of Senses (blue bistro, red space, and future branches), served on their own subdomains. Each site sells the atmosphere of one branch, shows its menu as a page-turning flipbook, and takes reservations and messages into the CMS. Success: a visitor gets a feel for the room, opens the menu, and books.

## Brand Personality
Handmade, unhurried, affectionate. The brand already speaks through ink doodles, sketchbook still-lifes and candle-lit interiors; the site should feel like flipping through the owner's sketchbook: pasted photos, pen notes in the margin, drawings in the branch's own ink colour (red pen, blue pen).

## Anti-references
- Generic restaurant templates: full-bleed hero slider, centred "Welcome to" heading, icon-card grids.
- Luxury fine-dining black-and-gold.
- Editorial-magazine lane (italic display serif, mono labels, ruled columns).
- Childish scrapbook clip-art; the drawings are the brand's own, not stickers.

## Design Principles
1. The branch colour is the pen: every hand mark (notes, underlines, arrows, headings) is drawn in it, so each branch feels like its own notebook.
2. Photos are pasted, not boxed: prints with tape, slight tilts, overlaps.
3. Say less, in a human voice: short handwritten notes instead of marketing paragraphs.
4. Booking is never more than one tap away.
5. The menu flipbook is the centrepiece and keeps its page-turn behaviour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text; handwriting font only for short notes and headings, never long body copy; all motion respects prefers-reduced-motion; Vietnamese diacritics must render in every font.
