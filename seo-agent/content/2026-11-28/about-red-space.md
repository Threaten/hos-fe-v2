Red Space is a House of Senses restaurant at 146/6E Võ Thị Sáu, in Xuân Hòa ward, the part of Ho Chi Minh City most people still call District 3. We open at 4pm and close at 11pm.

The kitchen cooks Western comfort food with a Vietnamese accent. There are eleven pastas, two of them tossed in garlic and fish sauce; bánh mì with pâté chaud under a creamy mushroom sauce, or with seared Kobe beef and cheese; salads, soups, Kobe steak, pan-seared salmon and a goose breast we smoke ourselves. To finish, there is taro and coconut mousse, caramel flan and a homemade fruit yoghurt, with fruit teas on the side.

The dining room is lit by table lamps and paper lanterns, with drawings and posters on the walls. It suits a quiet dinner for two as well as a table of friends sharing half the menu.

Planning a birthday or a group dinner? Contact us beforehand and we will prepare a table that suits the occasion. We look forward to welcoming you.
